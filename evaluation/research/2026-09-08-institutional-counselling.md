# Institutional reality check: course discovery, advice and admissions

Researched: 2026-09-08. Author: Codex. Scope: public Australian university and
VTAC material, with Victorian examples relevant to Clara's fictional setting.
This is desk research, not interviews, an audit of staff systems, or verified
access to an institution's CRM. Sources describe their own services; they do not
establish a universal operating model. No real institutional facts are imported
into Kingsford's synthetic database by this research.

## What is supported by public sources

RMIT's Cyber Security degree page separates learning mode, study load, location,
intakes and student audience. Its selection criteria change with education
background; online learning resources do not remove its campus attendance
requirement. That is a concrete reason to model delivery mode and workload as
separate fields. [RMIT course page](https://www.rmit.edu.au/study-with-us/levels-of-study/undergraduate-study/bachelor-degrees/bachelor-of-cyber-security-bp355)

Monash describes entry checks spanning academic background, English, prerequisite
subjects and additional selection requirements. Requirements may appear on course
or handbook pages; a single ATAR value is not a complete assessment.
[Monash entry requirements](https://www.monash.edu/study/how-to-apply/entry-requirements)

VTAC distinguishes raw ATAR from selection rank after adjustments. A published
lowest selection rank describes a prior selection period, not a pre-set universal
threshold. A lower raw ATAR therefore need not imply exclusion. Clara's current
synthetic cutoff convention must not silently become a real admissions rule.
[VTAC ATAR and selection-rank explanation](https://vtac.edu.au/files/pdf/factsheets/the_atar_profile_explained.pdf)

Study@RMIT supports future-student questions about courses, applications, pathways
and dates, while current/enrolling-student administration is directed to Student
Connect. This illustrates role-specific service routing, not one omniscient
“counsellor.” [RMIT contact channels](https://www.rmit.edu.au/contact/contact-us)

RMIT's direct application guidance identifies academic records, CVs and possible
additional documents or selection tasks, followed by application assessment and
an offer if successful. This distinguishes self-reported exploratory advice from
an application supported by evidence. [RMIT direct application](https://www.rmit.edu.au/study-with-us/applying-to-rmit/local-student-applications/application-methods/direct-application)

Monash assesses credit using qualification level and relevance of previous study.
Credit/RPL is therefore an assessment route, not something a course-search match
can certify. [Monash credit assessment](https://www.monash.edu/admissions/credit)

Monash separates admissions enquiry channels and limits disclosure of application
details to the applicant. This supports explicit identity/access boundaries;
it does not reveal which employee sees which database fields.
[Monash admissions contacts](https://www.monash.edu/admissions/contacts)

## Working channel model — synthesis to validate with a partner

| Information | Public website / anonymous assistant | Human future-student adviser | Admissions assessor / authenticated application channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Course facts | Published course/handbook data: level, discipline, campus, delivery, load, intake, duration, published costs, requirements | Published facts plus any authorized internal guidance | Applicable course/intake/cohort rules and supporting assessment procedures |
| Personal context | Only facts supplied through this interaction or an explicitly linked profile | Facts disclosed in conversation; prior enquiries only where the institution's access and identification process permits | Submitted application and authorized supporting records |
| Academic evidence | Self-reported study, scores and experience unless verified by an integration | Can discuss evidence and request clarification; verification authority must be established | Records/documents relevant to the application, and assessment results within the role's authority |
| Exceptions and pathways | Published pathway rules and their conditions | Can explain published options or route a case for assessment | Evaluate evidence for equivalence, credit or exceptions under applicable delegation |
| Application history/status | Unavailable anonymously | Not assumed; access must be confirmed with institution | Available only through authorized systems and scope |
| Decision | Explain plausible options and limitations | Advise or escalate; do not assume offer-making authority | Formal application outcome through the institution's process |

The internal-guidance, enquiry-history and assessment-system columns are proposed
integration categories, not verified descriptions of RMIT/Monash staff access.
Neither a person nor an AI gets blanket access simply by being called a counsellor.
The important boundary is role, available evidence and authority, not human versus AI.

## Consequences for Clara's accepted defects

An informed adviser should distinguish “interesting option,” “appears compatible
with your disclosed background,” “needs assessment” and an actual admission offer.
This is our proposed product standard, inferred from the separation above; no claim
is made that every real adviser reliably follows it.

- School leaver shown a postgraduate course: inspect the specific entry routes;
  if all supported routes are unmet, exclude it from suitable matches. Do not
  universally exclude every postgraduate course solely by its label: published
  professional-experience routes may exist. Missing entry data stays unknown.
- Online-only student: evaluate campus/placement attendance independently of load.
  A course with online resources is not necessarily accessible without attendance.
- Human contact: route to the right service and preserve qualifications, constraints,
  prior advice, evidence and unresolved questions. A shared summary does not imply
  that a handover or application submission actually occurred.

## Questions for a real institution, before adoption integration

Ask an admissions SME and a future-student adviser to walk through the same four
synthetic journeys. For each judgment: which source/system was consulted, what
fields were visible, what was self-reported versus verified, what could they decide,
and what required escalation? Request redacted workflow examples, not applicant data.

Confirm the owner of each course/eligibility field; update cadence and effective
intake; formal meaning of score thresholds; equivalence/experience routes; absence
versus incomplete data; CRM/history access rules; identity checks; case routing;
summary fields and resolution feedback. Confirm whether public and internal sources
can disagree and which takes precedence. Record uncertainty rather than inventing
an integration contract from public websites. No interviews have been conducted.
