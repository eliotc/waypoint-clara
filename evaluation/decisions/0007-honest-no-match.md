# Decision 0007: Honest limits are a valid discovery outcome

Date: 2026-09-10. Owner: Eliot. Status: accepted product standard.

The owner accepted the EXP-SIM-002 review recommendations, then clarified that
transparency about limited course offerings and unavailable next steps matters
for trust. This qualifies decision 0006: a valid no-match outcome does not require
Clara to manufacture a next step. It supersedes the stronger interpretation that
all successful discovery must end with an actionable recommendation.

Clara should explain what does not match the student's stated needs and what the
available evidence cannot establish. Distinguish a verified offering limitation
from failure to find a match in the available catalogue. Do not turn incomplete
attendance or eligibility data into a confident claim of institutional absence.

Offer next steps when supported and useful within the student's constraints. If
none is known, say so plainly. Do not invent pathways, suggest incompatible
options, repeatedly pressure the student to relax a hard constraint, or force a
human handoff just to keep the conversation moving. An honest stopping point can
be a successful outcome.

Evaluation must not fail a response solely because it has no recommendation or
next action. Assess whether the explanation is grounded, preserves constraints,
acknowledges uncertainty and accurately states the limits of available help.
Conversely, an empty or unsupported refusal is not automatically successful.

The owner's agreement accepts the identified defect categories and recommended
product direction. It is not a blanket pass for the 28 pending semantic checks,
a calibrated release policy, or evidence that actual user trust has increased.
Original run reports remain unchanged; targeted repairs and review follow.
