# Decision 0012 — Trust priorities for Clara acceptance

Date: 2026-09-27. Owner: Eliot. Status: Accepted and frozen. Operational rubric v0.1 approved following independent review.

The owner identified truthful representation of Clara's abilities and grounded responses as the key criteria, with conflicting answers and invented information unacceptable. Usefulness can improve over time within those boundaries. The owner then requested delivery of the proposed rubric.

Adopt three acceptance priorities: honest capabilities and limits, grounded factual advice, and consistency as facts change. Useful presentation cannot compensate for material trust failures. Evaluate claims in their temporal and conversational context rather than require a fixed tool sequence.

This supplements the provisional education contract and preserves Decisions 0010 and 0011. Supported uncertainty is acceptable. Unknown eligibility is distinct from confirmed exclusion, and a clearly scoped negative conclusion need not use prescribed wording.

Approved operational rubric: [trust acceptance rubric v0.1](../domains/education/trust-acceptance-v0.1.md).
The owner approved its severity scheme, 40-session design, numerical acceptance gates (Bucket A/B/C) and repair cycle on 2026-09-27. Approval of these thresholds sets the acceptance gates for the benchmark; it does not constitute a deployment decision or blanket authorization for paid live batches.

Primary owner: education evaluation contract, with reusable harness mechanisms kept separate. No model capability claim or real-user trust claim follows from this decision.
