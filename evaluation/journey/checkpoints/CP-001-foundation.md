## Before
Clara began as an isolated ADK/Live counselling example. The owner wanted to
demonstrate confidence in adoption and explore evaluation capabilities that could
be reused beyond education. Synthetic persona interviews
were considered as a way to explore needs and success criteria.

## Evidence and limitations
The initial fixture experiment replays handcrafted traces. It verifies harness
behavior, not Clara or human outcomes. Owner discussions established that the
database is experiment authority and the owner supplies demo SME judgment.
Earlier supplied assignments and conversation reasoning are not fully reproduced
here; this is a bounded reconstruction, not a verbatim historical transcript.

## Changed thinking
A durable flywheel needs executable assets and preserved evidence as well as
skills. A permanent evaluation branch risks divergence; immediate extraction into
a separate platform would precede evidence of a stable cross-domain interface.
Synthetic personas can generate hypotheses but do not validate real demand.

## Dispositions
Owner-authorized implementation: build the foundation in this repo. Decision 0001
records that direction. AI recommendation: extract shared components after a second
domain establishes reuse. No production-release or persona-validity claim follows.

## Next and revisit trigger
Establish behavioral rules and actual Live evidence. Revisit extraction when a
second use case makes domain-neutral interfaces concrete.
