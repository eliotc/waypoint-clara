## Before
Grounding instructions, online-course expectations, recommendation context,
handover and timing requirements contained unresolved product judgments.

## Evidence and limitations
Owner discussion accepted five provisional rules; local prompts and manual
expectations were aligned. This was specification work, not evidence of adherence.

## Changed thinking
Database evidence governs university claims. Sufficient context depends on the
next decision, not a fixed fact count. A human-contact recommendation must not
claim an unsupported transfer. Timing targets need observed baselines first.

## Dispositions
Owner acceptance: the five demo rules in decision 0002. Severity and release gates
remain proposed. No blanket acceptance of semantic evaluation results occurred.

## Next and revisit trigger
Exercise the real Live path. Revise clarification boundaries and performance
targets with evidence; revisit the rules if adoption scope or domain changes.
