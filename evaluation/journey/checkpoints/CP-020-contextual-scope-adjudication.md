# CP-020 — Reference labels need contextual review too

Recorded: 2026-09-22. Contemporaneous owner adjudication recorded by Codex.
Predecessor: CP-019 *(withheld)*.
Sources: [Decision 0011](../../decisions/0011-contextual-scope-in-evaluation.md), adjudication v2 *(withheld)*.

## Before

C15's no-matching-options recap was labelled an overbroad exclusion; both automated judges accepted it, one with high confidence. C14's eligibility assertion was also disputed.

## Evidence and limitations

Re-reading C15 shows that its opening scopes the answer to Cloud Computing. Merely retrieving Data Analytics does not prove relevance to the student's goal. C14, however, contains no explicit paid/professional duration disclosure; the defence introduced a fact not stated. No new model executions occurred.

## Changed thinking

Accept reasonable contextual scope rather than demand exhaustive disclaimers about every search result. The prior interpretation of C15 as a high-confidence error is superseded. This does not erase uncertainty distinctions or relax Decision 0010. Judges' agreement alone cannot establish why they reached their conclusions.

## Dispositions

Owner agreed: C15 supported in context, C14 retains unsupported status under the existing policy. Record changed references separately; preserve historical labels and outputs. Judge agreement was recomputed separately against the revised references. C05/C12 remain provisional/disputed; no full-benchmark approval.

## Next and revisit trigger

Use Decision 0011 in the next versioned rubric and test new scope-sensitive examples. Revisit if a purportedly narrow statement actually rules out the student's broader active goal, or if the owner revises professional-experience clarification. No runtime migration or additional paid batch is implied.
