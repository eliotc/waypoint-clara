# CP-010 — standalone value and visible limits before human integration

Recorded on: 2026-09-09. Provenance: contemporaneous. Author: Codex (AI-assisted).
Predecessor: [CP-009](CP-009-spoken-fidelity.md).
Sources: owner standalone-service hypothesis and agreement, persona/UI guidance
request; [decision 0006](../../decisions/0006-standalone-discovery.md);
persona pilot *(withheld)*.

## Before
Handover omissions led to a proposed structured handover record as the next step.
The owner questioned whether useful initial discovery could support adoption
without integrated human handoff and requested clear UI guidance about reliability.

## Evidence and limitations
Owner accepted testing that boundary. Added a persistent fictional-demo/eligibility
notice, expandable purpose/limits guidance and aligned agent instructions.
The first attempt stopped when automatic approval review hit a usage limit;
after the owner asked to continue, saved edits were resumed and validation completed.
The persona pilot is a constructed text experience: four synthetic personas, two
variants, separate model calls. Eight responses completed; none are real interviews
or actual use of Clara. Detailed observations and limitations are in the pilot review.

## Changed thinking
A successful initial outcome may be discovery plus a student-owned next-step recap,
not a transfer. Good disclosures alone do not establish useful progress. Simulated
responses recognize unverified eligibility in both variants because even the control
has a caveat; a banner effect cannot be claimed. Skeptical responses question value
when too much verification remains, while others ask for concrete next checks.
Those are hypotheses to explore, not validated adoption findings.

## Dispositions
Owner accepts deferring integrated handoff, keeping grounded contact available,
and using persona exploration plus UI guidance. This supersedes CP-009's proposed
next priority while preserving the handover defects as unresolved historical evidence.
No assertion that UI guidance eliminates overtrust. No push or deployment.

## Next and revisit trigger
Test useful self-contained discovery recaps and follow-up understanding, including
conflicting AI advice and collapsed/expanded UI states. Keep simulator inferences
separate from persona facts; validate with real users when possible. Revisit human
integration when necessary to fulfill the chosen service promise.
