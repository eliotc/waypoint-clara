## ATAR and Entry Requirements

### Understanding ATAR
The Australian Tertiary Admission Rank (ATAR) is a percentile rank between 0.00 and 99.95. It compares your results with all other Year 12 students in Australia. Most Kingsford undergraduate degrees have an ATAR cutoff, but this is the **lowest ATAR that received an offer in the previous year** — not a minimum requirement.

### Alternative Entry Pathways
You don't need a high ATAR to study at Kingsford. Alternative entry options include:
- **TAFE/VET pathway**: Complete a relevant TAFE certificate or diploma to gain entry
- **Mature-age entry**: Applicants aged 21+ can apply based on work experience and a personal statement
- **Foundation Studies**: Kingsford's own Foundation program for students who don't meet direct entry requirements
- **Special Entry Access Scheme (SEAS)**: For students whose ATAR was affected by personal, family, or health circumstances
- **Portfolio entry**: Available for Digital Media and some Arts programs

## English Language Requirements
For international students, minimum English requirements are:
- **IELTS**: Overall 6.5 (no band below 6.0)
- **TOEFL iBT**: Overall 79 (writing 21, speaking 18)
- **PTE Academic**: Overall 58 (no communicative skill below 50)

Students who don't meet these requirements may enter via the Kingsford English Language Program (KELP), a 10–20 week intensive pathway.

## Deferral Policy
Domestic students who receive an offer may defer their place for up to 12 months. International students should contact the International Admissions team to discuss deferral eligibility. A deferral fee of $500 applies.

## Medicine Degrees and Graduate Pathways
Kingsford University does not offer a Bachelor of Medical Studies, Doctor of Medicine (MD), or any direct-entry medical degrees. Prospective medical students can instead enrol in our Bachelor of Nursing, Bachelor of Public Health, or Bachelor of Psychology as high-quality preparation. Kingsford acts as a supportive "feeder" institution, providing undergraduate coursework and academic advising to help graduates apply for postgraduate graduate-entry medicine programs at nearby universities, such as the University of Melbourne and Monash University.

## Traditional Engineering Programs
Traditional engineering disciplines, including Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Electrical Engineering, are not offered at Kingsford University. We do not have programs in these traditional heavy engineering areas. Instead, Kingsford's engineering faculty is highly focused on modern, digital-first fields. We offer specialised, industry-aligned programs such as the Bachelor of Software Engineering (Hons), Bachelor of Computer Science, Bachelor of Cybersecurity, and the Graduate Certificate in Cloud Computing.

## Credit Transfer and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)
If you have completed prior studies at another university, TAFE, or registered training organisation (RTO), or have relevant professional work experience, you may be eligible for credit toward your Kingsford University degree. This is known as Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) or Credit Transfer.

### Key Details and Limits
- **Maximum Credit**: You can receive credit for up to **50% of the total credit points** of your Kingsford course (e.g., maximum 1.5 years of credit for a 3-year bachelor degree).
- **Study Currency**: Prior studies must typically have been completed within the last **5 years** to be eligible for credit.
- **How to Apply**: Submit your credit application alongside your course application. You must provide official academic transcripts, grading scales, and detailed course outlines/syllabi for the subjects you have previously completed.
- **Assessment Timeframe**: RPL applications take approximately **2–3 weeks** to assess once all documentation is received.

## Online Science Degrees Availability
Kingsford University does not offer online science-focused degrees. All science, health, and lab-based programs, including the Bachelor of Nursing, Bachelor of Public Health, and Master of Occupational Therapy, require significant on-campus practical laboratory sessions, clinical simulations, and in-person professional placements. Consequently, these programs are only offered in **Full-time, on-campus study mode** at our Melbourne campus to meet accreditation and training requirements.
